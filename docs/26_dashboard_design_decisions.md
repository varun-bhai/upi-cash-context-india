# Dashboard design decisions

The dashboard deliberately avoids the generic “AI SaaS” look. It is styled as
an **editorial payments fieldbook**, not as a product landing page.

## Choices we made

- A warm paper background, ink-like text, and thin rules replace white cards,
  bright gradients, and glass effects.
- `Source Serif 4` is used for analysis headings; `IBM Plex Sans` and `IBM
  Plex Mono` distinguish ordinary copy from source/date/metric labels.
- The opening section is a left-aligned research question and data window, not
  a centred marketing hero.
- The four key readouts are a ruled information strip rather than identical
  rounded KPI cards.
- Charts remain the visual focus. Decoration is limited to data labels, rules,
  and a restrained palette whose colours have consistent meaning.
- Limitations are kept in a compact “definition & caveat ledger,” because a
  credible data portfolio should visibly explain what a number does not mean.

## What we explicitly avoided

- Purple gradients, glassmorphism, decorative illustrations, floating shadows,
  and the generic three-card SaaS layout.
- Vague marketing copy such as “transform,” “unlock,” or “insights at your
  fingertips.”
- Fake precision, invented scores, or visual claims that UPI caused cash use
  to fall.

The result should feel like a thoughtfully made analyst’s working document:
specific to the topic, readable, and a little more restrained than a generic
startup dashboard.
