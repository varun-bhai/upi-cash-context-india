Definition & caveat ledger
  • PhonePe state data are a detailed app-level proxy, not all-UPI state totals.
  • NFS ATM withdrawals are a national cash-access proxy, not total cash use or total ATM activity.
  • The cash-to-demand-deposit ratio is an annual monetary-context measure, not cash spending and not proof that UPI caused a change.
  • Business labels are questions for investigation using public aggregates—not spending or product decisions.
